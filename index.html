Workshop overview

Beyond Multimodal

Omnimodal AI integrates human, environmental, digital, and physical information to understand and interact with people in context.

Building on the 2026 BrainLink X-Lab ↗, this workshop brings the CHI community together to refine emerging challenges and define what HCI should build, study, and evaluate.

Workshop goals

Develop a shared HCI research agenda and catalyze collaboration across HCI, AI, XR, accessibility, robotics, and related communities.

Preliminary Grand Challenges

01

Implicit human experience — interpreting uncertain human states.

02

Authority and safety — balancing reliability, risk, and permission.

03

Attentional intervention — deciding when AI should act or remain silent.

04

Tacit knowledge — capturing and sharing embodied expertise.

05

Perceptual influence — distinguishing augmentation from manipulation.

06

Ownership and accountability — clarifying data rights and responsibility.

02 / CFP
Call for papers

Join the Workshop

We invite 2–4 page position papers or case studies on human-centered omnimodal AI, including ongoing research, emerging systems, methodological challenges, and open questions.

Prior work explicitly using the term “omnimodal AI” is not required. We welcome contributions from HCI, AI, XR, multimodal interaction, wearables, robotics, accessibility, and related communities.

Topics of interest

01

Understanding human experience

Interpreting human states, behavior, attention, intention, and experience from multimodal signals.

02

Supporting human capabilities

Augmenting perception, learning, expertise, decision-making, accessibility, and collaboration.

03

Enabling human agency

Supporting human control over AI authority, intervention, data, accountability, and action.

Dates and submission details will be announced soon. All deadlines will follow Anywhere on Earth (AoE).

04 / SCHEDULE
Tentative program

Schedule

The workshop combines invited perspectives, participant lightning pitches, interdisciplinary discussion, and collaborative agenda-building activities.

Three interconnected HCI themes

THEME 1

Understanding human experience

How increasingly perceptive AI systems interpret human states, behaviors, attention, and experience.

THEME 2

Supporting human capabilities

How omnimodal systems can augment perception, learning, expertise, decision-making, and accessibility.

THEME 3

Enabling human agency

How people can understand AI decisions, guide its behavior, and accept, change, or reject its suggestions and actions.
